 GLOSSARY
 Tracking Error
 The tracking error measures the volatility of a portfolio’s relative return in relation to its benchmark index.
 Volatility
 An asset’s volatility is the standard deviation of its return. As a measure of dispersion, it evaluates the uncertainty of asset prices, which is often equated to
 their risk. Volatility can be calculated ex post (retrospectively) or estimated ex ante (anticipatively).
