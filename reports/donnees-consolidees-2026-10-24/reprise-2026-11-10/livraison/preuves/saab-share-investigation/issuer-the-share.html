The share

The Saab B share is listed on Nasdaq Stockholm.

The series B shares have one vote each while the series A shares have ten votes each. The total number of shares is 543,383,388 of which 9,535,612 are A shares and 533,847,776 are B shares. All A shares are unlisted and owned by Investor AB.
