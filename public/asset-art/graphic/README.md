# Comparatif ETF — style 2

`comparison-style-2.webp` is the image-generation preview selected by the user on
7 October 2026: cream background, saturated blue/green/orange panels and 3D scenes.
It is stored locally so PNG exports do not depend on a remote image service.

The Canvas renderer samples only the illustrations, below the preview headings.
Names, fees, ISINs, performance years and values are rendered from the application's
existing data. The preview's World / S&P 500 / Nasdaq labels are never exported.
The world, American skyline and technology scenes are selected by exposure; other
themes reuse the existing relevant 3D assets. Metal funds keep their own metal art.

The approved visual is converted to WebP for delivery; no financial data is baked
into the illustration layer. No series heading or footer is added.
