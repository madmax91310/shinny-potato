# Performance depuis — approved glass studio

Approved by the user on 4 October 2026. The design follows the transparent glass investor portraits: asset showcase on the left, title, period, cumulative return and discrete annual cards on the right. Cards reflow into up to four columns; canvas height follows the real annual count. Comparison uses only common years and separate asset panels. No line, interpolation or invented monthly path is drawn.

`performance-gold-glass.webp` is decorative artwork produced with ImageGen from the user-approved gold reference. All fixed text, dates, percentages, credits and bullion engravings were removed. Numerical data and attribution are drawn independently by Canvas.

Other exposures reuse reviewed `etf-night` studio illustrations: geographic objects for indices, sector objects for companies without a verified symbol, bonds for bond series, silver for silver. `performanceArt.js` explicitly covers every market asset. Eight identities reuse verified SVG silhouettes with metallic shading; no issuer symbol is substituted for an index. Pictures are illustrative and do not encode investment weights, constituents or returns.

The source credit stays on the image; the Performance depuis tweet text omits it as requested. Annual return calculation and currencies are unchanged.
