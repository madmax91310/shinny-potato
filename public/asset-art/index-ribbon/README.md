# Index ribbon illustrations

Eight bespoke exposure scenes for the 16 index factsheets. Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 4 October 2026, then resized to a maximum of 1200 × 800 and encoded as WebP (quality 88). The `.webp.b64` files contain only the base64 WebP payload; `loadIndexArt` decodes them on demand and caches the decoded image.

Art direction: brushed copper sculptural ribbon, purple glass enterprise blocks, controlled studio highlights, dark aubergine background. These are illustrations, not quantitative diagrams, provider logos, or literal constituent representations. Only nominal index names appear as numerals in the relevant artwork. Real counts, company names, weights, dates and returns are drawn separately from the shared data registry.

Reviewed subjects:

- `sp500`: sculptural 500, S&P 500 universe including Equal Weight.
- `nasdaq`: sculptural 100, technology-inspired glass enterprise blocks.
- `europe600`: sculptural 600, European enterprise architecture.
- `europe50`: sculptural 50, euro area enterprise architecture.
- `small`: sculptural 2000, small American enterprise blocks.
- `japan`: abstract ribbon, rising sun and Japanese architecture; no number, usable for TOPIX and Nikkei 225.
- `world`: abstract ribbon around a globe; no number, usable for global and developed-market geographic variants.
- `emerging`: abstract ascending ribbon, Asian architecture and semiconductor-inspired blocks; no number.

Geographic and methodology variants are identified by the exact sourced index name, exposure description, countries and holdings. Do not reuse a numbered scene for an index with another nominal universe. Do not turn decorative block sizes into percentages.
