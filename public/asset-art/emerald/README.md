# Investment emerald artwork

Reviewed 2026-10-04. `apple.webp` is the selected emerald-glass Apple mockup,
without baked-in text, dates, prices, charts or signature. The bitten-apple
silhouette and detached leaf were checked visually against the existing Apple
identity review in `../README.md`. This image is exclusive to the `apple` ID.

`studio.webp` is the matching empty emerald-glass pedestal, used behind exact
vector silhouettes. Prompt: dark emerald studio, empty circular optical-glass
pedestal in the left third, subtle gold caustics; right two thirds and lower
third empty, no subject, logo, text, numbers or chart. Both images were generated
with the built-in image tool and converted to local 1600×1040 WebP.

`emeraldArt.js` retains the reviewed Microsoft, Tesla, Broadcom, ASML, Bitcoin
and Ethereum SVG contours unchanged; only surface shading, extrusion and light
are applied. Microsoft remains the four-square corporate mark. The shared
identity map supplies matching illustrations for indices, metals, sectors and
companies without a reviewed corporate mark. These scenes are illustrations,
not official logos. Berkshire uses the holding illustration, not a bond scene
or the logo of Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices. A custom asset gets a neutral
holding illustration. An unknown ID rejects instead of reusing Apple.

The data and simulation formulas remain in the shared calculator. Monthly
observations use a capital curve; annual observations use actual year-end
capital columns. A custom two-price calculation shows only its two endpoint
values. Annual charts never turn interpolated monthly prices into observations.
Currencies, DCA contributions, entered final prices and source credits remain
explicit. Gold retains its mandatory source/licence credit; silver retains its
futures scope. No FX conversion is introduced.

CI checks all asset IDs in both contribution modes, source-file presence,
unchanged results, currency, clipping/overlap, long amounts, losses, custom
endpoints, annual observations and image-load failure/retry. Representative
PNGs are available in `investment-visual-checks`.
