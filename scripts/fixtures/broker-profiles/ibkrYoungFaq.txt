<html><body><main><h1>Who can open a PEA Account?</h1><p>Any French individual tax resident over the age of 18 can open a Plan d&#x27;épargne en actions \(PEA\) Account, provided he or she does not already own one, and is fiscally independent. There can only be two PEA Accounts within a single tax household – the taxpayer’s and his/her spouse’s.</p><p>In addition, PEAs are always individual accounts \(no joint PEA can be opened\).</p><p>_The material contained herein is for general information only and is not intended to serve, and should not be construed as, tax, legal or investment advice.  Please consult your own advisors._</p></main></body></html>
