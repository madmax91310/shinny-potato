MSCI World Index (EUR)

The MSCI World Index captures large and mid cap representation across 23 Developed Markets (DM) countries*. With 1,249 constituents,
the index covers approximately 85% of the free ﬂoat-adjusted market capitalization in each country.
For a complete description of the index methodology, please see Index methodology - MSCI.
