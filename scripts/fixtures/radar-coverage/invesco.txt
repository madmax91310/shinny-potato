                                 Invesco forges new highs and launches new ETFs
                                 with support from UK-based wealth manager


London, 01 October 2026 – Invesco’s European ETF platform has reached several key milestones in
2026, with total platform assets surpassing $200 billion, and its swap-based ETF range breaking
through $100 billion in assets under management (AUM). The most recent addition to this platform –
the Invesco MSCI North America Swap UCITS ETF – was launched at the end of May 2026 and has,
in a few short months, become the latest fund in the firm’s market-leading swap-based range to
exceed $1 billion in AUM, alongside the largest swap-based ETF in the world and some with unique
exposures to key indices.


UK wealth manager Quilter were the lead investors in this new ETF via its Cirilium Blend and Passive
multi-asset portfolios, as well as for the Invesco Global Government Bond UCITS ETF, launched even
more recently. Quilter was seeking differentiated, cost-effective investment solutions for its client
platform and needed a trusted partner with expertise across several asset classes and investment
styles, and one that could grow products at scale.


CJ Cowan, portfolio manager of Quilter’s Cirilium portfolios, said: “We are always looking for
ways in which we can access core markets in ways that are value additive for our investors. These
marginal gains may be small but over time they add up and give a nice boost to portfolios. The North
America ETF gives us a tax-efficient way to gain exposure to companies in the region in a low-cost
format, making it ideal for inclusion in the Cirilium Passive and Cirilium Blend multi-asset ranges.”


About the Invesco MSCI North America Swap UCITS ETF


The Invesco MSCI North America Swap UCITS ETF is currently the only fund on the market that uses
a swap-based method to replicate the MSCI North America Index. An ongoing charge of 0.08% per
annum makes the ETF the lowest-cost exposure to this index, which comprises currently 620 large
and mid-cap companies, covering 85% of the free float-adjusted market capitalisation of the US and
Canada.


“Invesco pioneered the multi-counterparty swap model in 2009”, explained Matt Tagliani, Head of
EMEA ETF Product at Invesco, “and for more than 16 uninterrupted years, our investors have
benefitted from low tracking and access to opportunities in which our swap-based model has a
structural advantage. One clear example is for indices involving US equities, including the MSCI North
America index, where this approach can gain a material advantage over physical replication by paying
a lower withholding tax rate on dividends.”


Since 2009, Invesco’s swap-based platform has become increasingly robust, streamlined and
efficient, processing more than 300,000 swap resets and over 100,000 creations and redemptions
representing $600 billion in value. The firm was the first in the industry to offer a multi-counterparty
swap model, which together with strict controls on counterparty exposure, frequent swap resets, and
rigorous counterparty oversight have all contributed to delivering a resilient counterparty risk
management model that builds investor trust.


About the Invesco Global Government Bond UCITS ETF


The Invesco Global Government Bond UCITS ETF aims to replicate the performance of the
Bloomberg Global Aggregate Treasuries Index. The index comprises fixed-rate, local currency
government debt issued by investment-grade countries in developed and emerging markets, with the
largest currency exposures in the index being USD (36%), EUR (23%), JPY (13%), CNY (11%) and
GBP (6%).


The Invesco ETF has an ongoing charge of 0.09% per annum, making it one of the lowest-cost
exposures to a broad basket of global government bonds. Investors wanting to reduce the foreign
currency risks may wish to consider GBP- or EUR-hedged share classes, which are available for
0.12% per annum.
