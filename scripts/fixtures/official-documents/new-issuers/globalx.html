Key Information

ETF Summary

The Global X Nasdaq 100 Covered Call UCITS ETF (QYLD LN) follows a synthetic strategy, in which the Fund seeks to replicate a buy-write index by selling covered calls, with underlying equity exposure designed to match constituents of the Nasdaq-100 Index.

ETF Objective

The Global X Nasdaq 100 Covered Call UCITS ETF (QYLD LN) seeks to provide investment results that correspond generally to the price and yield performance, before fees and expenses, of the Cboe Nasdaq-100 BuyWrite v2 UCITS Index.
