  Fund Description                                                                          30 September 2026

  VanEck Gold Miners UCITS ETF is a UCITS-compliant exchange-traded
  fund (ETF) that invests in a portfolio of equity securities. The ETF aims to
  provide investment returns that closely track, before fees and expenses,
  the performance of the MarketVector™ Global Gold Miners Index
  (MVGDXTR).
  The MarketVector™ Global Gold Miners Index is a pure-play, global index
  designed to track the performance of the largest publicly traded
  companies worldwide that are primarily involved in gold and silver mining,
  including mining, royalty, and streaming activities. The index is a modified
  free-float market-capitalisation-weighted index and is reviewed and
  rebalanced on a quarterly basis using a transparent, rules-based
  methodology.
  Further information on the index is available at www.marketvector.com
