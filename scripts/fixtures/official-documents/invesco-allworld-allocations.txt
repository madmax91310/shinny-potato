Fund objective
The Invesco FTSE All-World UCITS ETF Acc aims to
achieve the net total return of the FTSE All-World Index (the
"Reference Index"), less the impact of fees.
An investment in this fund is an acquisition of units in a
passively managed, index tracking fund rather than in
the underlying assets owned by the fund.
