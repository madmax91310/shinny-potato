Fund objective
The Invesco NASDAQ-100 Equal Weight UCITS ETF Acc
aims to achieve the net total return performance of the
NASDAQ-100 Equal Weighted Index (the “Reference
Index”), less the impact of fees.
An investment in this fund is an acquisition of units in a
passively managed, index tracking fund rather than in
the underlying assets owned by the fund.
