Trading Central expand UCITS range with two more quantamental equally weighted UCITS ETFs

  • Trading Central has expanded their range of UCITS ETFs with the launches of Trading Central Quant U.S. 50 Equity Index UCITS ETF (ticker: TCQU) and Trading Central Quant Global 50 Equity Index UCITS ETF (ticker: TCQW).
  • The ETFs aim to provide systematic exposure to 50 select securities based on their quantamental ratings.
  • The TCQU ETF will track the Solactive TC Quant U.S. 50 Index and the TCQW ETF will track the Solactive TC Quant WW 50 Index, built on Trading Central’s proprietary framework.
  • The ETFs are eligible for the French PEA account.
  • The ETF will list on Xetra, Borsa Italiana, Euronext Paris, and London Stock Exchange.

When you invest in ETFs your capital is at risk.

These further releases mark Trading Central’s continued expansion into investable products, supporting self-directed retail clients with its award-winning, proprietary financial research and data.

The ETFs aim to replicate the performance of the Solactive TC Quant U.S. 50 Index and the Solactive TC Quant WW 50 Index respectively.

The Solactive TC Quant Indices are built on Trading Central’s proprietary TC Quantamental Rating® framework. This rules-based, systematic strategy scores eligible securities across five factor groups: growth, value, income, quality, and momentum to select the top 50 securities. The indices are equally weighted, capped to 17 securities per sector, and rebalanced monthly to reduce concentration risk.

The breadth and balance of factors in this data-driven approach aims to help lower volatility and better risk-adjusted returns over time, which aligns with the goals of many long-term investors.

The ETFs employ physical replication, and the ETFs are eligible for the French PEA account.
