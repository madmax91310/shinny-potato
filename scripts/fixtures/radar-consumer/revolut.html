Revolut receives French banking licence

Revolut Bank S.A. (RBSA) has been granted a full banking licence, following a joint assessment by the Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution (ACPR) and the European Central Bank (ECB), with the decision formally adopted by the ECB Governing Council, marking an historic milestone in Revolut's European journey. Revolut already serves about 30M customers across Western Europe, with close to 8 million joining in 2025 alone. The French banking licence strengthens the group's position as one of Europe's largest retail banks.
